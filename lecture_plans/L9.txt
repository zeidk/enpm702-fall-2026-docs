            <h3><i class="icon-rubric-dark"><span class="dp-icon-content" style="display: none;">&nbsp;</span></i>Unit Objectives</h3>
            <p>This lecture covers advanced object-oriented programming in C++. Building on the OOP fundamentals from Lecture 8, it explores how classes relate through association, aggregation, composition, and inheritance, then introduces compile-time and runtime polymorphism with <code>virtual</code> methods, <code>override</code>, and <code>= default</code>. It examines virtual destructors, abstract classes with pure virtual methods, and the <code>final</code> keyword.</p>
            <p class="whitespace-normal break-words">By the end of Lecture 9, students will be able to:</p>
            <ul class="[&amp;:not(:last-child)_ul]:pb-1 [&amp;:not(:last-child)_ol]:pb-1 list-disc space-y-1.5 pl-7">
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Understand class relationships: association, aggregation, composition, and inheritance</li>
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Distinguish compile-time vs. runtime polymorphism and use <code>virtual</code> methods</li>
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Apply the <code>override</code>, <code>= default</code>, and <code>final</code> keywords correctly</li>
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Choose between <code>auto</code> and explicit base types for polymorphic objects</li>
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Implement <code>virtual</code> destructors to prevent resource leaks</li>
                <li class="whitespace-normal break-words">Create abstract classes with pure <code>virtual</code> methods and concrete implementations</li>
            </ul>
